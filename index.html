Why Shardline
Better prices through splitting

Most DEXes route your entire order through one pool. Shardline splits it across multiple pools to minimise price impact and improve your fill.

Shard routing

Your order is split across deep, core, and thin liquidity pools using an optimal allocation algorithm.

Less price impact

Spreading the input across pools keeps each individual pool's impact low — the math always beats a single-pool route.

One transaction

The router contract bundles every shard into a single on-chain call. One signature, one gas fee.

Slippage guard

Set your tolerance and the router reverts the whole swap if the combined output falls below your floor.
